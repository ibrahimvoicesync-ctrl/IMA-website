# Design Context

## Users

The primary buyer is a **head of marketing at an AI or developer-tools brand**. They land on this page from a referral, a creator post, or a cold email — usually while evaluating who else is doing creator marketing in this space. They are skeptical (most influencer agencies waste their time and budget) and they read fast. They will leave if the page sounds like a generic agency.

**Job to be done:** in under 60 seconds, decide whether InityX is worth a reply.

**Primary action:** the buyer fills out the form (or emails directly) to **get in contact** with the founder. This is *not* a "book a call" funnel — there is no calendar embed, no discovery flow, no automation. It is a direct human inquiry.

## Brand Personality

Three words: **honest, determined, technical**.

- **Honest** — won't fake case studies, won't promise vanity metrics, will say "we're not the right fit" out loud. Tone is plainspoken, lightly opinionated.
- **Determined** — small team, picky about who they take on, genuinely committed to the AI/dev-tools category.
- **Technical** — speaks the buyer's language (LLMs, evals, MCP, RAG, dev tooling). Not "tech-themed," actually technical.

**Voice rule:** if a sentence could appear on any agency site, rewrite it. Specifics over slogans. "We pick 4–8 creators and ship in 4–6 weeks" beats "We deliver impactful campaigns."

## Aesthetic Direction

**Reference site:** [qlivens.netlify.app](https://qlivens.netlify.app/) — split-screen hero, condensed display headlines with a single italic-serif accent line, sticky-scroll process column, dark-bg "real talk" admission section, beige content surfaces. The current InityX page mirrors that build-up.

**Anti-references:** generic dark-mode SaaS pages with cyan/purple gradients, glassmorphism, and sparkline decorations. No "AI agency" tropes (orbiting rings, neon grids, animated nebulas).

**Theme:** alternating light/dark by section. Beige owns the content sections (services, approach, contact). Deep purple owns the dramatic moments (hero left, feature service card, real-talk). The contrast between the two surfaces is the page's signature.

**Color palette (locked):**
- `--beige` `#f1e8d6` — primary light surface
- `--beige-dark` `#e6dbc4` — alternate light surface
- `--purple-deep` `#2b1b3d` — dominant dark surface
- `--purple-mid` `#4d2e6e` — hover state
- `--purple-bright` `#6e44a3` — accent on light surfaces
- `--purple-accent` `#b794f4` — lavender accent on dark surfaces
- `--ink` `#160e22` — footer near-black
- `--muted` `#756680` — muted body text on beige

**Typography:**
- Display headlines: **Bebas Neue** (condensed all-caps, dramatic scale)
- Italic serif accent inside headlines: **Bodoni Moda** italic (editorial high-contrast, more distinctive than the DM Serif default)
- Body: **Hanken Grotesk** (humanist grotesk with personality, not the Inter/DM Sans reflex)

## Design Principles

1. **Plainspoken, not decorated.** Copy and visual choices both. If a flourish doesn't carry meaning, cut it.
2. **Two surfaces, full commitment.** Beige sections and purple sections each go all-in on their tone. Don't muddy them with in-between greys.
3. **Editorial scale.** Headlines are Bebas Neue at 4–8rem, body is calm and readable. The size jump between display and body should feel like a magazine spread, not a SaaS dashboard.
4. **The italic line is sacred.** One Bodoni italic line per headline, no more. It's the moment of voice in a sea of condensed caps.
5. **Get in Contact, not Book a Call.** Every CTA points to direct human contact via the form or email — no calendar, no funnel.
