Process

How
It
Works

A direct process built around what actually matters: getting the right creators in front of your product and measuring real outcomes.

01

Discovery & Fit

Reach out via email or book a meeting directly. We dig into what you've shipped, who's already using it, what good looks like for this campaign, and what would make it a waste of money. No deck — just questions.

02

Creator Research

From our roster of AI and developer-tool creators, we shortlist the 6–10 who actually fit your product and audience. You see real engagement data — not vanity follower counts — before we reach out to anyone.

03

Outreach & Brief

We hand each creator a tight brief — what to demo, what to skip, what metric matters. They draft in their voice; you approve before anything ships. No surprises at launch.

04

Campaign Execution

Coordinated rollout across channels — long-form first, clips after. We handle scheduling, links, UTMs, and creator-side troubleshooting so your team stays focused on the product.

05

Performance Review

One report. Reach, qualified signups, retained users at 30 days, cost per qualified signup. If a creator underperformed, we say so — and we don't book them again unless we have a reason.
