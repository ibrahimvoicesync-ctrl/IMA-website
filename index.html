Services

01 — Core

Fixed-Fee Campaigns

One agreed scope, one agreed price, defined start and end. You pay upfront and we deliver the full creator push end-to-end. Best for one-off pushes where you want cost certainty.

02

Monthly Retainers

A recurring monthly fee in exchange for ongoing creator work. We operate as your creator team month after month — planning content, learning what works, and adjusting as we go. Best for teams that want creator marketing as a steady channel.

03

Performance-Based

You only pay for results. We run the campaign with little or no upfront fee, and earn through a share of the revenue, sign-ups, or other outcomes we drive. Reserved for established brands we've qualified — strong product, clear demand, real conversion.

Process

How
It
Works

A direct process built around what actually matters: getting the right creators in front of your product and measuring real outcomes.

01

Discovery & Fit

Reach out via email or book a meeting directly. We dig into what you've shipped, who's already using it, what good looks like for this campaign, and what would make it a waste of money. No deck — just questions.

02

Creator Match

We already have a list of creators ready to go — people who make videos about AI and tech tools, hand-picked by us. When you tell us about your product, we choose the ones whose fans will care most. You see all the real numbers about each creator — views, likes, comments, who their fans are — before we message anyone.

03

Outreach & Brief

We hand each creator a tight brief — what to demo, what to skip, what metric matters. They draft in their voice; you approve before anything ships. No surprises at launch.

04

Campaign Execution

Coordinated rollout across channels — long-form first, clips after. We handle scheduling, links, UTMs, and creator-side troubleshooting so your team stays focused on the product.

05

Performance Review

One report. Reach, qualified signups, retained users at 30 days, cost per qualified signup. If a creator underperformed, we say so. And if a campaign falls short of what we promised you, we will try to make it right at our cost — extra posts, additional content, or organic follow-up until we land where we said we would. Your outcome is on us.
