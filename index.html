— Process

From kickoff to live in five steps.

  1. 01
    Week 0 · Discovery

    We learn your product.

    30-minute call. We dig into what you've shipped, who's already using it, what "good" looks like for this campaign, and what would make it a waste of money. No deck, no pitch — just questions.

  2. 02
    Week 1 · Strategy

    We pick the right creators.

    From our roster of AI/dev creators, we shortlist the 6–10 who actually fit your product and audience. You see real engagement data — not vanity follower counts — before we reach out to anyone.

  3. 03
    Week 2 · Brief

    Creators draft, you approve.

    We hand each creator a tight brief — what to demo, what to skip, what metric matters. They draft in their voice; you approve before anything ships. No surprises at launch.

  4. 04
    Weeks 3–5 · Launch

    Content goes live.

    Coordinated rollout across channels — long-form first, clips after. We handle scheduling, links, UTMs, and creator-side troubleshooting so your team stays focused on the product.

  5. 05
    Week 6 · Report

    We tell you what actually worked.

    One report. Reach, qualified signups, retained users at 30 days, cost per qualified signup. If a creator underperformed, we say so — and we don't book them again unless we have a reason.
